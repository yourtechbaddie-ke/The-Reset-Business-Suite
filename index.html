01 / THE BRIEF

START WITH THE PROBLEM

THE ASK

Wellness content is everywhere. Most of it asks people to become a better version of themselves.

The Reset takes the opposite position: you don't need another optimisation routine. You need a small interruption that lets you return to the day as yourself.

WHOAlways-on professionals
Creatives / young teams
TENSION“I know I should slow down.”
“I don't have time to.”
JOB TO DOMake a ten-minute pause feel useful, desirable and doable.
SUCCESSStop the scroll → earn attention → give one simple next action.
